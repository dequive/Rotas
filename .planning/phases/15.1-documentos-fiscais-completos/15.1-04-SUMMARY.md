# Phase 15.1 Plan 04: Wave 2A — Nota de Débito + Nota de Crédito (FDOC-02, FDOC-03) Summary

## One-liner

`POST /billing/documents/{id}/debit-note` and `POST /billing/documents/{id}/credit-note` endpoints creating child fiscal documents with sequential invoice numbers and parent linkage.

## What Was Built

- `create_debit_note()` service function — creates `document_type='debit_note'`, assigns invoice number, links `parent_document_id`
- `create_credit_note()` service function — creates `document_type='credit_note'` with negative amount
- Both routers at `POST /billing/documents/{id}/debit-note` and `POST /billing/documents/{id}/credit-note` (status 201)
- `CreateDebitNoteRequest` and `CreateCreditNoteRequest` schemas
