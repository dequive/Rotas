# Phase 15.1 Plan 05: Wave 2B — Fatura-Recibo, Recibo + AR (FDOC-04, FDOC-05) Summary

## One-liner

`POST invoice-receipt`, `POST receipt`, and `GET /billing/ar` with runtime AR aging computation in 5 buckets.

## What Was Built

- `create_invoice_receipt()` — creates `document_type='invoice_receipt'`, transitions parent to `paid`
- `create_receipt()` — creates standalone `document_type='receipt'` without parent modification
- `list_ar_documents()` — returns issued documents with `due_date` set, ordered by `days_overdue DESC`
- `serialize_billing_document()` extended with computed `days_overdue` (int) and `aging_bucket` (str)
- `GET /billing/ar` endpoint with optional `?aging_bucket=` filter
- `CreateReceiptRequest` schema
